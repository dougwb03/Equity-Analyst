Dell Technologies Delivers Third Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results


ROUND ROCK, Texas — Nov. 24, 2026 — Dell Technologies (NYSE: DELL) announces financial results for its fiscal 2027 third quarter and provides guidance for its fiscal 2027 fourth quarter and full year.

Third-Quarter Summary
•Record revenue of $54.9 billion, up 103% year over year
•Record diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $8.82, up 287% year over year, and record non-GAAP diluted EPS of $9.45, up 265%
•Record third-quarter cash flow from operations of $4.5 billion
•Full-year FY27 revenue guidance of $202.0 billion, up 78% year over year
•Full-year FY27 diluted EPS guidance of $28.24 and non-GAAP diluted EPS guidance of $29.68, up 225% and 188% year over year, respectively

“We expect record full-year revenue of $202 billion, up 78%. We are now a different company, with Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG) driving the vast majority of our profit and growth,” said Jeff Clarke, vice chairman and chief operating officer, Dell Technologies. “We ended the quarter with a record $132.3 billion AI backlog after booking $60.2 billion of orders, each rigorously vetted for customer quality and access to capital, land and power. Customers turn that backlog into revenue sooner because we test the complete rack before it ships and stand it up within six hours of delivery, which shortens time-to-first-token. Demand continues to accelerate and broaden globally, with more than 7,500 customers deploying Dell AI Factories.”

“The team executed exceptionally well, driving record revenue of $54.9 billion, record EPS and a record $4.9 billion returned to shareholders,” said David Kennedy, chief financial officer, Dell Technologies. “Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue continues to decline, while non-GAAP operating expenses fell to 7% of revenue, the lowest in our 42-year history. We’ve continued to run the company more efficiently as we’ve grown, and that is driving higher profitability, even as AI servers become a larger part of our business. We’ve generated record year-to-date cash flow from operations of $10.8 billion, and the Board approved a $25 billion increase to our repurchase authorization, reflecting confidence in long-term value creation.”

Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG)
•Record revenue: $42.0 billion, up 198% year over year
•Record AI-Optimized Servers revenue: $22.0 billion, up 291% year over year
•Record Traditional Servers and Networking revenue: $15.0 billion, up 235% year over year
•Record third-quarter Storage revenue: $4.9 billion, up 24% year over year
•Record operating income: $7.1 billion, up 308% year over year

Capital Return
Dell Technologies returned a record $4.9 billion to shareholders in the third quarter through share repurchases and dividends.

The Company’s Board of Directors has also approved the repurchase of an additional $25.0 billion of shares of the Company’s Class C common stock under the stock repurchase program previously approved by the Board of Directors.
