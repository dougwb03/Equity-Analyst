## Investment Recommendation

**Rating: Hold**

Costco’s fiscal 2026 results demonstrate exceptional fundamental strength, with net sales of $297.2 billion and a robust return on average equity of 28.4%. The company’s conservative balance sheet, evidenced by a debt-to-equity ratio of just 17.2%, provides significant financial resilience.

**Deciding Number:** 48.4 (Price-to-Earnings Ratio)

**Main Support:** The primary support for the stock lies in its sustained operational efficiency and capital allocation. The 28.4% ROE and $9.382 billion in free cash flow underscore Costco’s ability to generate shareholder returns through dividends and buybacks without over-leveraging the balance sheet.

**Main Risk:** The key limitation is the elevated 48.4 P/E ratio. This multiple implies high future growth expectations that may not be sustainable, particularly if the 20.9% growth in Digitally-Enabled sales fails to scale proportionally or if macroeconomic headwinds compress consumer spending. Until the valuation re-rates lower or earnings growth accelerates significantly to justify the premium, the risk of downside correction outweighs the upside potential.
